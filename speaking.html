English Speaking Practice

Learn useful English sentences and practice speaking every day.

Listen, repeat, and speak these sentences aloud.

1. Basic Greetings

Hello.

سلام.

Hi.

سلام.

Good morning.

صبح بخیر.

Good afternoon.

بعد از ظهر بخیر.

Good evening.

عصر بخیر.

How are you?

حال شما چطور است؟

I am fine, thank you.

من خوب هستم، تشکر.

See you later.

بعداً می‌بینمت.

Goodbye.

خداحافظ.

2. Introducing Yourself

My name is Ahmad.

نام من احمد است.

I am from Afghanistan.

من از افغانستان هستم.

I live in Sweden.

من در سویدن زندگی می‌کنم.

I am a student.

من یک شاگرد هستم.

I work here.

من اینجا کار می‌کنم.

I am learning English.

من انگلیسی یاد می‌گیرم.

I speak a little English.

من کمی انگلیسی صحبت می‌کنم.

I want to improve my English.

من می‌خواهم انگلیسی خود را بهتر کنم.

3. Meeting Someone

روز من خوب می‌گذرد.

It is good to see you.

خوشحالم که شما را می‌بینم.

Have a nice day.

روز خوبی داشته باشید.

28. Daily Speaking Challenge

Introduce yourself in English.

Say your name.

Say where you are from.

Say where you live.

Say what you do.

Say something about your family.

Talk about your daily routine.

Talk about your favorite food.

Talk about your hobbies.

Talk about the weather.

Talk about your plans for tomorrow.

Try to speak for one minute without stopping.

Speaking Practice Method

Step 1: Read the English sentence.

Step 2: Listen to the sentence.

Step 3: Repeat the sentence aloud.

Step 4: Repeat it three times.

Step 5: Try to say it without looking.

Step 6: Use the sentence in a real conversation.

Practice for at least ten minutes every day.

English Listening

Listen to simple English sentences and practice understanding them.

Lesson 1: Simple Sentences

Good morning.

صبح بخیر.

How are you today?

امروز چطور هستید؟

I am learning English.

من در حال یادگیری زبان انگلیسی هستم.

I like this book.

من این کتاب را دوست دارم.

Where do you live?

کجا زندگی می‌کنید؟

I live in Sweden.

من در سویدن زندگی می‌کنم.

Practice

Listen to the sentences and repeat them aloud.

Good morning. — How are you today? — I am learning English. — I like this book. — Where do you live? — I live in Sweden.

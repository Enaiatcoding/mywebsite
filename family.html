Family in Sentences

This is my father.

این پدر من است.

This is my mother.

این مادر من است.

I have one brother.

من یک برادر دارم.

I have two sisters.

من دو خواهر دارم.

My family is big.

خانواده من بزرگ است.

My family is small.

خانواده من کوچک است.

My parents live with me.

والدین من با من زندگی می‌کنند.

My brother is young.

برادر من جوان است.

My sister is a student.

خواهر من یک شاگرد است.

I love my family.

من خانواده‌ام را دوست دارم.

We are a happy family.

ما یک خانواده خوشحال هستیم.

Who is your father?

پدر شما کیست؟

Who is your mother?

مادر شما کیست؟

How many brothers do you have?

چند برادر دارید؟

How many sisters do you have?

چند خواهر دارید؟

Family Practice

Say these words aloud:

Father — Mother — Parents — Son — Daughter

Brother — Sister — Child — Children

Grandfather — Grandmother — Grandson — Granddaughter

Uncle — Aunt — Cousin — Nephew — Niece

Husband — Wife — Partner — Married

Father-in-law — Mother-in-law — Brother-in-law — Sister-in-law

Stepfather — Stepmother — Stepbrother — Stepsister

Now practice these sentences:

This is my father.

This is my mother.

I have one brother.

I have two sisters.

My family is big.

I love my family.
